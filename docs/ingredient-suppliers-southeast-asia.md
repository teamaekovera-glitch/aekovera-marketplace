# Ingredient Suppliers — Southeast Asia

**Load-bearing assumption:** Association membership and official-site evidence (certifications, export markets, capacity claims) are treated as sufficient for "credible supplier" status at prototype stage; a live audit (buyer references, lab reports, sample approval) remains mandatory before onboarding any of these into a transacting catalog. The adjacent explanation this research skips: several profiled companies are self-publishing their own claims — where no second source exists, that is marked, not hidden.

*Prepared for the Aekovera iNGREDIENT marketplace. All URLs below were retrieved this session (September 19, 2026, UTC). Every factual claim carries the URL of the page it came from. Facts not found are marked **unknown** — never inferred.*

## Method and genuineness rules applied

1. **Candidates** came from web searches across: Vietnam coffee/pepper/cashew/cinnamon-star anise, Indonesia coconut/cocoa/spices/coffee, Philippines coconut/fruit, Thailand tapioca/coconut milk/fruit powders, Malaysia palm fats, Cambodia rice/pepper, plus association and trade-body leads (VICOFA, VINACAS, Vietnam Pepper and Spice Association, Kampot Pepper Promotion Association).
2. **Verification** = the supplier's official website was fetched and read this session. Where an association/registry page added cross-evidence and was fetched, it is cited as a second source.
3. **Certifications** are recorded only as claimed on fetched pages, with the page URL. Where a site has a certificates section whose names were not present in the retrieved text, this is recorded as "certificates referenced; names not captured in retrieved text — unknown."
4. **MOQs, sample policies, prices** are recorded only where published. Almost all bulk ingredient pricing in this region is quote-only; the two exceptions found are labeled with their tier: `supplier-published` and `marketplace-listed`.
5. **Review presence:** no independent public review platforms were found for any profiled supplier in this session's checks; first-party testimonials on supplier sites are labeled as first-party and are not counted as reviews.
6. **Dropped candidates** (researched leads not included) are listed with reasons in the final section.

**Verification levels used:** `Official-site` = official domain fetched this session. `Official-site + association context` = additionally cross-referenced against an association page fetched this session.
